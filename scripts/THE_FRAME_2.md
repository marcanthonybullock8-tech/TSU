# THE FRAME 2: THE VERDICT
### TSU Film 9 — Release Phase 2, Film 3 — the "Captain America: The Winter Soldier" release slot

---

## THE PITCH

**Platform:** THE FRAME, a second time — still Arianna Cummings's, still run on her own terms per the acquisition settled in `scripts/THE_ASSEMBLY.md`, now discovering that the parent company protecting it has been quietly compromised from the inside for years.

**Why a second THE FRAME fits the Winter Soldier slot exactly:** Winter Soldier is about discovering the institution you trusted has been corrupted from within since before you ever joined it, a friend presumed lost turning out to be a weapon someone else built, and a villain who is trusted specifically because no one thought to distrust him. THE ROSTER absorbed THE FRAME in `scripts/THE_ASSEMBLY.md` on good terms, in good faith — which is exactly the kind of institutional trust this film is built to test. Evelyn Marchetti-Hale has sat on this franchise's boards since `scripts/THE_PROJECTOR.md`, "right roughly half the time," per her own bible entry — precisely the kind of person nobody thinks to suspect the other half of the time.

**Who this film belongs to:** Arianna Cummings and Marc-Anthony Bullock together — this is the first film since `scripts/THE_ROSTER_3.md` to genuinely co-lead two founders, because the threat sits inside infrastructure they both depend on. **Jules Abernathy-Kim** (`TSU_Extended_Ensemble.md`, "The Press") gets her first real activation as the Sam Wilson position — an outside ally with her own expertise, not a founder, not for sale. **Benji Oyelaran-Cox** (`TSU_Extended_Ensemble.md`, "The Wildcards" — introduced as a nobody in Phase 1, built to "age up across the whole timeline in real time") fills the Bucky Barnes position: a presumed-vanished young technologist, quietly isolated and used for years by the very institution everyone trusted. Priya Ramasubramanian fills the Nick Fury position, including its central trick.

**In-universe timeline:** **January 2014 – June 2014.** Ages verified against `TSU_Core_Cast.md`: Marc-Anthony turns 32 on Jan 5; Arianna is 31 the entire film (turns 32 in October, after the story closes); Esther is 31, turning 32 in September.

## LOGLINE

THE FRAME's discovery feed has started quietly burying creators before they've done anything wrong — and when Arianna Cummings and Marc-Anthony Bullock pull the thread, they find an algorithm running across every platform Evelyn Marchetti-Hale has ever sat on the board of, built to predict and preemptively silence "risk" before it happens, maintained for years by a brilliant young engineer everyone in this franchise assumed had simply disappeared.

## SYNOPSIS

It's early 2014. Arianna Cummings notices something small first — a handful of THE FRAME creators, all promising, all suddenly invisible in the discovery feed for no reason anyone can find in the platform's own published rules. Marc-Anthony notices the same pattern at THE ROSTER a week later. Neither company's engineers can explain it. Something is deciding who gets seen before either platform's own systems ever weigh in.

**Priya Ramasubramanian**, quietly investigating a pattern of oddly-timed platform decisions across her entire portfolio for months, gets too close. Her car goes off a coastal road on the way home from a meeting nobody else knew she'd taken. The reports call it a serious accident. The prognosis is grim. The franchise's connective tissue since Film 1 — the corkboard, the meetings, the "I want them in one room" — goes dark overnight, and Arianna and Marc-Anthony are left with no one steering the bigger picture but themselves.

What they find, working together for the first time as genuine equals, is **THE VERDICT** — a predictive-suppression system running quietly beneath THE FRAME, THE ROSTER, and at least two other platforms, all of which share exactly one board member: **Evelyn Marchetti-Hale**. THE VERDICT doesn't moderate what's already happened. It predicts what a user is statistically likely to do or say next — based on financial stress signals, mental health flags, associations, sentiment trends — and suppresses them pre-emptively, silently, permanently, before they've done anything at all. Evelyn built it, over years, genuinely convinced she was protecting the industry and the public from the next scandal, the next harassment mob, the next Feed War, by removing the risk before it could metastasize. She is not wrong about the dangers she's seen up close for a decade. She is catastrophically wrong about the solution.

The system's actual technical backbone, it turns out, has been maintained for three years by **Benji Oyelaran-Cox** — the teenage prodigy who did uncredited early engineering work on THE FRAME itself back in `scripts/THE_FRAME.md`, and who everyone in this ensemble assumed had simply moved on to other work when they quietly stopped showing up. In reality, Evelyn found Benji isolated, financially desperate, and offered exactly the kind of validation and purpose a brilliant, overlooked young person is hardest to protect from — years of it, slowly, until Benji believed completely that THE VERDICT was necessary, protective work, not a censorship machine with no oversight and no appeal.

Working with journalist **Jules Abernathy-Kim** — the one byline every founder in this franchise checks first and dreads most, already circling rumors of THE VERDICT for months without a source willing to confirm it — Arianna and Marc-Anthony trace the system to a live rollout: Evelyn is about to expand THE VERDICT industry-wide, sold to the boards she sits on as "predictive trust and safety," on the same week as a major cross-platform transparency summit. Confronting Benji directly, rather than exposing them alongside Evelyn, is what actually breaks the case open — Benji, finally shown genuine, unconditional support instead of validation with strings attached, hands over the system's full architecture and its complete suppression logs.

At the summit, Arianna and Marc-Anthony expose THE VERDICT publicly, with Jules's reporting running in the same hour — and Priya, alive the entire time, quietly protected and hidden by Constance Abiodun-Mayhew while she gathered the financial trail connecting Evelyn's board seats, reveals herself on stage at exactly the moment Evelyn insists there's no proof. THE ROSTER, THE FRAME, and every platform Evelyn touched commit publicly to independent oversight and a real appeals process. Evelyn is removed from every board she sits on and referred to Senator Ashworth-Okafor's committee. Benji is not prosecuted — rehabilitated, publicly credited for coming forward, and quietly welcomed back into the ensemble's orbit rather than discarded, the same mercy this franchise has extended to every genuinely remorseful person it's put through the wringer.

## THE ANTAGONISTS

Per the Villains Bench rules (`TSU_Continuity_Bible.md` §5), the real cause traces to a person's decision, made with the sincere belief it was protective.

1. **EVELYN MARCHETTI-HALE**, escalated from her recurring role since `scripts/THE_PROJECTOR.md` (the Alexander Pierce analog) — genuinely convinced THE VERDICT protects the industry from harm she's watched up close for a decade. Not a mustache-twirler; the single most dangerous kind of villain this franchise has produced, because she is right about the danger and catastrophically wrong about the cure.
2. **THE VERDICT**, THE ENGINE's ninth and most totalizing manifestation yet — not reactive moderation, predictive suppression: judgment rendered before an act occurs, with no appeal and no visibility, the clearest possible argument yet for why an algorithm can never be trusted with unaccountable power.
3. **BENJI OYELARAN-COX** *(the Bucky Barnes analog, not a villain)* — a genuinely brilliant, genuinely overlooked young technologist, isolated and slowly convinced rather than coerced by force. The film's insistence on rehabilitation over prosecution is deliberate: Benji was a weapon someone else built, the same way Teddy Ashcombe-Vance's bible entry always specified corrosion "grown there rather than planted."

## HOW THIS FILM CONTINUES RELEASE PHASE 2

- **A recurring gatekeeper's ambiguity, finally cashed in as real danger:** Evelyn Marchetti-Hale's bible entry always said she was "right roughly half the time" — this film is what the other half looks like at scale.
- **A long-dormant Wildcard activated exactly as designed:** Benji Oyelaran-Cox's entire purpose per `TSU_Extended_Ensemble.md` was to "age up across the whole timeline in real time" and eventually become "quietly indispensable" — this is the film where that indispensability turns out to have a cost nobody was watching for.
- **The Press, finally load-bearing:** Jules Abernathy-Kim's bible entry called her "an actual check on power" from the moment she was created — this is the first film to actually need one.
- **Priya's Nick Fury role, played all the way through:** not just the connective corkboard, but the fake-out at the center of the source film — she was never actually gone, and the ensemble (and audience) believing otherwise is the point.
- **Institutional consequence at real scale:** for the first time, a villain's fall changes how every platform in the franchise operates going forward, not just one company.
