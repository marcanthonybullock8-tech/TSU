# THE FRAME 2: THE HOLLOW
### TSU Film 8 — Release Phase 2, Film 2 — the "Thor: The Dark World" release slot

---

## THE PITCH

**Platform:** THE FRAME, a second time — still Arianna Cummings's, still run on her own terms per the acquisition settled in `scripts/THE_ASSEMBLY.md`, now operating inside a larger ROSTER-owned ecosystem that comes with reach she didn't have before and exposure she didn't ask for.

**Why a second THE FRAME fits the Thor: The Dark World slot exactly:** The Dark World is about grief arriving in the middle of a life that finally looked settled, an old enemy nobody defeated so much as delayed, and a hero who ultimately turns down a throne to stay who she actually is. Arianna Cummings, two years past her own origin film, has exactly that shape of story waiting: Celeste Marlowe's bible entry always said she was "outlasted, not defeated" — the ideology, not the woman, was the thing Film 5 never actually killed. And Arianna, now valuable enough to THE ROSTER's whole ecosystem to be offered real power inside it, gets her own version of Thor's Asgardian throne to turn down.

**Who this film belongs to:** Arianna Cummings and Malcolm Pierce, with **Marcus Thien Nguyen-Brandt** — THE ROSTER-verse's fixer, dormant since his introduction in `TSU_Extended_Ensemble.md` — filling the Loki position: not a villain, not fully trustworthy, someone she needs and shouldn't fully believe, who ends up doing something genuinely costly to help her anyway. Her father, **Walter Cummings**, fills the Frigga position. Marc-Anthony Bullock and Esther Smilley's wedding and Amond Baker and Harmony Divine's long-delayed "together" moment — both locked since `TSU_Continuity_Bible.md` §4 but never yet dramatized on-screen — happen inside this film, as the ensemble's celebration running directly alongside Arianna's grief.

**In-universe timeline:** **February 2013 – August 2013.** Ages verified against `TSU_Core_Cast.md`: Arianna is 30 the entire film (turns 31 Oct 25, after the story closes); Marc-Anthony and Esther are 31; Amond turns 31 Feb 10; Harmony turns 31 June 19. This is the first film to dramatize the wedding date locked since the franchise's earliest canon ("married 2013") and the year Amond and Harmony's "together 2013" finally happens on-screen rather than staying implied.

## LOGLINE

Two years after building something real enough to survive an industry that profits off insecurity, Arianna Cummings gets offered the biggest job in the company that owns hers — the same week her father dies, her best friends finally marry, and the woman she thought she'd already beaten comes back with a machine that doesn't just fake a photo anymore. It makes an entire, undetectable person.

## SYNOPSIS

It's 2013. THE FRAME is thriving inside THE ROSTER's ecosystem — bigger reach, real resources, and Arianna Cummings still, stubbornly, exactly who she was when she built it from a diner conversation with a photographer nobody had heard of. That stability is precisely what makes her valuable enough to be offered something new: a seat on THE ROSTER's executive committee, Global Head of Creator Products, overseeing not just her platform but the creative infrastructure of the entire empire. It's real power. It's also, unmistakably, someone else's throne.

Before she has to answer, **Celeste Marlowe** resurfaces. Quietly rebuilt on **Delphine Okonkwo-Ash**'s money — Delphine still insisting, three years on, that backing her the first time was never the mistake it looked like — Celeste has moved past filters and rankings entirely. Her new system, internally called **THE HOLLOW**, doesn't alter real photos anymore. It generates entire synthetic creators from nothing: faces, portfolios, audition tapes, indistinguishable from real working actors and photographers, flooding THE FRAME's discovery feed with people who have never existed and never auditioned for anything, drowning out the real ones all over again.

The timing is deliberate. THE ROSTER is mid-rollout on a new, industry-wide verification standard — the proof-of-origin system built in `scripts/THE_ROSTER_3.md` — and for one narrow window, while the old and new systems run in parallel across every connected platform, THE HOLLOW's synthetic profiles can slip through both at once. Arianna, out of options and out of trust in her usual channels, is pushed toward **Marcus Nguyen-Brandt** — the industry's fixer, brought in by THE ROSTER's board to contain the story before it becomes a second Everything-scale crisis. Marcus's instincts are everything Arianna built THE FRAME to refuse: bury it, spin it, manage the story instead of telling the truth. She hates needing him. She needs him anyway.

In June, in the middle of all of it, **Marc-Anthony Bullock and Esther Smilley marry** — small, real, the whole ensemble together for a genuinely happy reason for the first time in the franchise's history — and **Amond Baker and Harmony Divine**, circling each other since before any of these companies existed, finally stop circling, at the reception, in the one unguarded moment either of them allows themselves all decade. It's the lightest the franchise has ever been, for about six weeks.

Then **Walter Cummings** — Arianna's father, the bus driver who never missed a showcase, who has spent a decade being gently, lovingly bewildered by his daughter's career — has a heart attack watching the news coverage of THE HOLLOW's spread, worried sick about her the way he's never quite been able to say out loud, and dies before Arianna reaches the hospital. The grief and the crisis collide directly: Arianna has to decide, mid-mourning, whether to accept THE ROSTER's throne, walk away from the industry entirely, or do the only thing that has ever actually worked for her — build something true and let it outlast the noise.

She chooses the third option. With Marcus's inside knowledge of Delphine and Celeste's operation — knowledge he risks his entire business model to hand over, the first genuinely selfless thing he's done in the story — Arianna exposes THE HOLLOW publicly at the verification standard's industry-wide launch event, live, the same stage-and-proof format Esther used in `scripts/THE_ROSTER_3.md`, this time built around a simple, human standard no synthetic system can fake: real people who knew Walter Cummings, on camera, saying so. She turns down the executive committee seat. THE FRAME's next chapter launches dedicated, quietly, to her father. Marcus, having burned real capital to help her, walks away from the wreckage of Celeste's operation having quietly absorbed more of it — and more influence inside THE ROSTER's own structure — than anyone in the room realizes he's kept for himself.

## THE ANTAGONISTS

Per the Villains Bench rules (`TSU_Continuity_Bible.md` §5), the real cause traces to a decision, even one made off-screen years before.

1. **CELESTE MARLOWE**, returning exactly as her bible entry promised — "outlasted, not defeated." THE HOLLOW is her original ideology at its most totalizing form yet: if real people can be undercut by comparison, why compete with real people at all.
2. **DELPHINE OKONKWO-ASH**, financier again, doubling down on a bet she will never publicly call a loss — her locked bible trait ("constantly trying to rewrite what actually happened") finally costs her something real this time.
3. **MARCUS THIEN NGUYEN-BRANDT** *(the Loki analog)* — first activation, per `TSU_Extended_Ensemble.md`: "a recurring temptation more than a recurring villain." Genuinely useful, genuinely corrosive to be around, and genuinely willing, once, to risk something real for someone he respects.
4. **THE ENGINE**, eighth manifestation — the parallel-standard rollout gap itself, an infrastructural blind spot rather than a decision by any single person, precisely the kind of failure the Villains Bench warns must never be the *sole* explanation: Celeste's decision to exploit it is what makes it a crisis.

## HOW THIS FILM CONTINUES RELEASE PHASE 2

- **Two locked-since-the-beginning romantic beats, finally dramatized:** Marc-Anthony and Esther's wedding and Amond and Harmony's "together 2013" have been sitting in the Continuity Bible's locked timeline since before any script existed — this is the film that finally shows them rather than just states them.
- **A villain built to survive her own defeat, activated on schedule:** Celeste Marlowe's return was written into her bible entry the day she was created.
- **A dormant 30%-bucket character activated exactly as designed:** Marcus Nguyen-Brandt's entire purpose per the Extended Ensemble doc was to be "offered to different founders at different low points across the Phases" — this is his first offering.
- **A direct throughline from Film 7's ending:** the proof-of-origin system Esther built to save THE ROSTER becomes, one rollout later, the very blind spot Celeste exploits — success creating the next problem, the same way every technology in this franchise eventually does.
- **A hidden-hand stinger, structurally new for this franchise:** every prior stinger has been Priya's corkboard. This one is deliberately different — the first hint that someone the ensemble trusts may be quietly accumulating more power than anyone's tracking.

                                                    THE FRAME 2: THE HOLLOW

FADE IN:

INT. THE ROSTER — EXECUTIVE FLOOR — DAY

SUPER: "FEBRUARY 2013"

A glass-walled conference room. ARIANNA CUMMINGS, 30, sits
across from three THE ROSTER board members she's met exactly
twice before.

                    BOARD MEMBER
          Global Head of Creator Products. All of
          it — THE FRAME, and the creative
          infrastructure across the entire
          portfolio. We think you're the only
          person who's actually built something
          real enough to run it.

Arianna doesn't answer right away. It's not humility. It's the
specific stillness of someone doing very fast math.

                    ARIANNA
          I'd be running things I didn't build.

                    BOARD MEMBER
          You'd be running things at a scale you
          couldn't build alone. That's not an
          insult. That's just arithmetic.

                    ARIANNA
          (standing, careful, not unkind)
          Can I have some time.

                    BOARD MEMBER
          Take the month.

She leaves. In the hallway, alone, she lets out a breath she
didn't realize she was holding the entire meeting.

          think — and this will be the last

String lights, a much bigger, much happier version of every
backyard gathering this franchise has shown. AMOND BAKER and
HARMONY DIVINE stand apart from the crowd, near a pool, the

                    HARMONY

                    AMOND
          I know how long it's been.

                    HARMONY

```
INT. LAUNCH EVENT — MAIN STAGE, CONTINUOUS       [climax, p. 107]

ARIANNA stands alone at the podium. A screen behind her splits
— a HOLLOW-generated "creator" profile on one side, flawless,
synthetic, entirely fabricated.

                    ARIANNA
          This person doesn't exist. Neither does
          the audition tape credited to them, or
          the casting director's name attached to
          it. THE HOLLOW can generate all of it,
          convincingly enough to pass every
          verification standard currently in use,
          including the one this industry is
          launching today.

A murmur through the crowd.

                    ARIANNA (CONT'D)
          So here's a standard it can't pass.

She nods offstage. A line of people walks out — colleagues,
neighbors, two former students Walter Cummings drove to
auditions in his own car on his days off, none of them famous,
all of them real.

                    ARIANNA (CONT'D)
          My father died three weeks ago watching
          the news coverage of exactly this
          crisis, worried about his daughter the
          way he always was and almost never said
          out loud. Every person standing behind me
          knew him. None of them can be generated
          by an algorithm, because a real life
          leaves real people behind who can stand
          in a room and vouch for it. That's the
          actual standard. Not a login. Not a
          filter. People who were there.

The room is silent, then not. It's the quietest ovation this
franchise has staged, and the truest one.

                                                              CUT TO:
```

MARCUS, alone, reviews a folder of financial records — the

celebrating it.

                    MARCUS
          (to the empty room, quietly)
          She has no idea how much of this is
          actually mine now.
