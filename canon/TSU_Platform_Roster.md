# THE SOCIAL UNIVERSE (TSU)
## Master Platform Roster — Fictional Counterparts

**Franchise Mandate:** Every real-world platform gets a fictional 1:1 analog — same cultural role, same rise/fall arc, same "powers" — built to be owned, merchandised, and continued past where reality stops. Faithful to the *spirit* of the source, not the *name* of it.

Organized like MCU Phases: founding era → expansion → fragmentation → the current cinematic battlefield.

---

## PHASE 0 — THE PRECURSORS (1997–2001)
*"Captain America" tier — the ones nobody remembers using but everyone owes a debt to.*

| Real Platform | Year | Real Role | Fictional Counterpart | Concept |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| SixDegrees.com | 1997 | First "profile + friends list" site | **THE LEDGER** | A stark, almost bureaucratic site that treated friendship like a legal registry — literally asked users to "notarize" connections. Ahead of its time, dead by 2001. Origin-myth status: the Uncle Ben of the universe. Everyone quotes its founder's manifesto; nobody used it. |
| Classmates.com | 1995 | Reunite old school contacts | **ROLL CALL** | Yearbook-themed, nostalgia-driven, quietly profitable forever by refusing to evolve. The "S.H.I.E.L.D. that never became an Avenger" — always in the background, funding things. |
| LiveJournal | 1999 | Confessional blogging + micro-communities | **DEAR DIARY** | Built by ex-poets, beloved by teenage goths and fandom communities. Becomes the site every other platform's writers secretly still use. Your Wakanda: hidden, culturally rich, underestimated. |
| GeoCities | 1994 | DIY personal homepages, "neighborhoods" | **HOMESTEAD** | Users build literal digital houses in themed "districts" (you had to pick a neighborhood to live in). Gloriously ugly, deeply personal. The site's shutdown becomes TSU's first "Snap" — an entire generation's homes erased overnight. |

## PHASE 1 — THE FOUNDING FOUR (2002–2004)
*Your Iron Man / Cap / Thor / Hulk. Each gets an origin film.*

| Real Platform | Year | Real Role | Fictional Counterpart | Concept |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Friendster | 2002 | First mainstream social network; collapsed under its own success | **THE FIRST CIRCLE** | Invented "the friend graph" as a status symbol. Founder is a visionary who refuses to add features fast enough — killed by his own perfectionism, not by a villain. Tragic-hero origin, your Bruce Banner: brilliant, cautious, ultimately consumed by what he built. |
| MySpace | 2003 | Customizable profiles, music/band culture, "Top 8" social hierarchy | **TOP8** | Explicitly named after its own most infamous feature — ranking your best friends publicly was social warfare. Music-scene launchpad, glitter-HTML chaos, an unelected mayor-of-the-internet figure everyone's first friend. Your Thor: brash, beloved, a little embarrassing, genuinely royal in its prime. |
| LinkedIn | 2003 | Professional networking | **THE BOARDROOM** | Everyone's there, nobody admits to checking it, deeply powerful in ways users don't respect until they need a job. Your Nick Fury: unglamorous, always working in the background, secretly runs everything. **No core-seven founder (locked) — already a mature "old guard" institution before Phase 1's story begins; cameo-only in `scripts/THE_ROSTER.md` scene 13.** |
| TheFacebook | 2004 | Harvard-exclusive, exploded into global dominance, founder disputes | **THE ROSTER** | Started as an industry-verification flex — verified working entertainment professionals only, a "who's actually real" layer before anyone called it that. Founder-dispute subplot intact (co-founder frozen out via cap-table dilution, a legal fight over origins) — but fictionalized enough to own outright. Your Tony Stark: arrogant, magnetic, built an empire and burned bridges doing it. **Founders (locked): Marc-Anthony Bullock, with Amond Baker diluted out as co-founder. TSU Film 1 ("Iron Man" release slot) — see `scripts/THE_ROSTER.md`.** |

**Phase 1 finale event:** *THE ROSTER* absorbs and outlasts *TOP8*, mirroring MySpace's real collapse — this is your "Battle of New York," the first time these platforms actually go to war over the same users.

## PHASE 2 — THE MEDIA WARS (2005–2010)
*Format specialists. Your Guardians of the Galaxy / Ant-Man tier — weirder powers, more fun.*

| Real Platform | Year | Real Role | Fictional Counterpart | Concept |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| YouTube | 2005 | Video hosting, "Broadcast Yourself" | **THE PROJECTOR** | Democratized video the way printing presses democratized text. Its origin story literally starts as a failed dating site pivot — perfect comic-tragic energy. Your Star-Lord: didn't mean to end up here, ends up defining the era anyway. |
| Reddit | 2005 | Anonymous forums, upvote democracy, niche communities | **THE HIVE** | A shapeshifting city of a thousand tiny nations (subreddits), governed by karma instead of currency. Volatile, occasionally heroic, occasionally a mob. Your Rocket Raccoon: chaotic, smarter than it looks, capable of real loyalty. |
| Twitter | 2006 | Real-time, 140-character public square | **THE WIRE** | Started as a "what are you doing" status tool, mutated into the planet's nervous system for breaking news and public meltdowns. Fast, addictive, structurally incapable of nuance. Your Quicksilver: everything happens through it before anyone can process it. **Founder (locked): Amond Baker, solo, post-ROSTER. TSU Film 2 ("Incredible Hulk" release slot) — a power built from his own wound that grows angrier and less controllable than he intended. Script not yet written.** |
| Tumblr | 2007 | Aesthetic microblogging, fandom/art hub, reblog culture | **THE GALLERY** | A wall that never runs out of wall. Fandom, art, activism, and chaos coexist in the same infinite scroll. Your Doctor Strange: a little mystical, attracts a specific kind of devoted believer, operates by different rules than everyone else. |
| Instagram | 2010 | Photo-first, filtered, aspirational | **THE FRAME** | Turns every user into a magazine editor of their own life. Origin as a bare-bones check-in app that got reduced to just photos + filters — addition by subtraction. Your Captain America (post-serum): became the platonic ideal by stripping everything down to its purest form. |

**Phase 2 finale event:** *THE FRAME* gets quietly acquired by *THE ROSTER* — your "Age of Ultron" beat: the empire absorbing its most dangerous young rival instead of fighting it.

## PHASE 3 — THE MOBILE INFINITY SAGA (2010–2017)
*The stakes go global. Your Infinity Stones era.*

| Real Platform | Year | Real Role | Fictional Counterpart | Concept |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Pinterest | 2010 | Visual mood-boarding, aspirational planning | **THE ATLAS** | A map of things you want your life to become. Quiet, huge, oddly powerful in commerce (it's where people plan weddings, renovations, everything) — underestimated because it's coded "unserious." Your Wanda Maximoff: soft-spoken, immensely powerful, nobody sees it coming. |
| WhatsApp | 2009 | Private, encrypted, global messaging | **THE WHISPER NETWORK** | No profiles, no feed, no performance — just a billion private conversations happening at once, invisible to the public platforms warring above it. Your Black Widow: works in the shadows, more essential to the outcome than anyone in the spotlight realizes. |
| Snapchat | 2011 | Disappearing content, intimacy through impermanence | **THE EMBER** | Everything it shows burns up on purpose. Built an entire language (streaks, stories) around things *not* lasting — the anti-Facebook. Your Vision: a genuinely new kind of being, born from a totally different philosophy than everything that came before it. |
| Vine | 2013 | 6-second looping video, killed too soon | **THE FLICKER** | Brilliant, beloved, gone in under two years — a supernova. Its death becomes TSU's actual "Infinity War snap": an entire generation of comedians orphaned overnight, several founding a diaspora that reshapes *THE PROJECTOR* years later. Your Quicksilver's death: shocking, formative, permanently mourned by the fandom. |
| Discord | 2015 | Voice/text communities, gaming-rooted, server culture | **THE OUTPOST** | Started as a tool for one specific tribe (gamers) and quietly became the internet's town hall of private nations — a thousand walled gardens nobody outside them can see into. Your Wakanda: hidden in plain sight, more technologically advanced than anyone gives it credit for, eventually forced onto the world stage. |

## PHASE 4 — THE ALGORITHM AGE (2018–PRESENT)
*The multiverse saga. Everything fragments, everything competes for the same eyeballs, nobody trusts the timeline anymore.*

| Real Platform | Year | Real Role | Fictional Counterpart | Concept |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| TikTok | 2016/18 | Algorithm-first short video, foreign-owned, geopolitical lightning rod | **THE ORACLE** | Doesn't ask what you want to see — *tells* you, with unsettling accuracy. Foreign-born, endlessly scrutinized by governments, impossible to look away from. Your Thanos: not evil, just inevitable, reshapes the entire landscape by sheer gravitational force. The Phase 4 antagonist. |
| BeReal | 2020 | Anti-algorithm, unfiltered, simultaneous posting | **THE MIRROR** | A rebellion platform built entirely as a rejection of THE FRAME and THE ORACLE — forces authenticity by removing the ability to perform. A resistance cell, not an empire. Your Shuri: young, idealistic, built specifically to fight what came before it. |
| Threads | 2023 | THE ROSTER's answer to THE WIRE's chaos | **THE ECHO** | Launched overnight by the empire (THE ROSTER) specifically to cannibalize a wounded rival (THE WIRE) — a direct, almost cynical act of corporate warfare. Your Ultron: created by the establishment specifically to destroy a threat, arguably becomes just as dangerous. |
| X (formerly THE WIRE) | 2023 | Ownership change, rebrand, chaos era | **THE WIRE, REBORN AS: SIGNAL-0** | The old public square, bought out and rebuilt by an outsider with his own agenda — same infrastructure, radically different philosophy, half its former population fled to rival platforms overnight. Your post-Thanos universe: familiar shape, fundamentally changed rules. |

## THE VILLAINS BENCH
*(Non-platform entities — services, trends, and forces that antagonize across every Phase)*

| Real-World Concept | Fictional Counterpart | Role |
|---|---|---|
| The Algorithm (generic) | **THE ENGINE** | Not a platform — the force *inside* every platform that decides what you see. TSU's Infinity Stones-equivalent: whoever controls it controls everything. Every platform has one; none of them admit how much power it has. |
| Data breaches / privacy scandals | **THE LEAK** | A recurring catastrophic event, not a character — TSU's Thanos-snap-adjacent disaster that hits a different platform every Phase, always with the same beat: trust breaks, users flee, someone testifies before Congress. |
| Influencer culture / MLM-adjacent grift | **THE SWARM** | A parasitic subculture that infects every platform in turn, reskinning itself each time (bloggers → YouTubers → Instagram influencers → TikTokers). Recurring Loki-tier trickster antagonist — never fully defeated, just rebranded. |
| Bots / fake engagement | **THE COPIES** | An army, not a villain — every platform's Chitauri. Swells right before a platform's downfall, always dismissed as harmless until it isn't. |
